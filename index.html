How the simulation works

Make offensive and defensive calls through the second half. Each call covers up to three snaps and pauses after a first down or possession change. Routine punts happen automatically; you choose on meaningful fourth downs. There is no decision limit. Use “Simulate the rest of the game” whenever you want automatic play-calling to finish from your current situation.

Opponent profiles use Sharp Football Analysis’s Week 7 efficiency rankings, captured Oct. 25, 2026. They blend success rate and EPA and adjust for opponents. They are not DVOA. This current snapshot applies to all three historical starting situations.

Passing, rushing, pass protection and explosive-play rankings change completion, sack, turnover and yardage probabilities. Repeating a specialized approach three times in your recent calls triggers a capped opponent adjustment. Mixed offense and mixed coverage remain flexible.

The probability mappings are designed for this game, not fitted NFL win probabilities. Two-point tries, field goals, timeouts, onside kicks and hurry-up decisions depend on the game situation. Ties end the challenge after regulation.
